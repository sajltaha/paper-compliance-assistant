# 2 Evidence of relevance

The selected method is secondary-data analysis. The full study, source register and derived requirements are in [DATA_ANALYSIS.md](DATA_ANALYSIS.md).

The study compares five official conference rule sets and six initial-submission profiles, analyses the published CHI 2025 screening counts, and synthesises six research sources from 2023-2025. It separates observed policy requirements and operational counts from proposed product behaviour and unmeasured user demand.

Published CHI counts include 495 desk rejections among 5,014 completed submissions; 229 cases fall into the reported anonymization, template and length categories. These figures support the relevance of submission checks in that conference process, without establishing a preventable fraction or a product accuracy estimate.

Eight proposed requirements address versioned profiles, section-aware counting, rule modality, traceable findings, qualified anonymity warnings, conversion-quality gates, criteria confirmation and evidence-grounded recommendations. No benchmark performance or interviews are claimed. See DATA_ANALYSIS.md references [A]-[N] for the sources and qualifications.
