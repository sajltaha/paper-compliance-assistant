# Materials for supervisor review

Show the prepared report and prototype. Ask the supervisor to review:

1. Target users and realistic MVP scope.
2. Sufficiency of scientific sources and validity of comparisons.
3. Go/Vue choices, proposed PostgreSQL storage and parser alternatives.
4. Acceptance criteria and the distinction between formal checking and semantic AI analysis.
5. Corpus, independent annotation, metrics and two baselines.
6. Permitted external manuscript processing and its limitations.

After the discussion, record actual comments in supervisor_feedback_log.csv. Link each comment to a decision and revision. This guide does not send anything to the supervisor.
