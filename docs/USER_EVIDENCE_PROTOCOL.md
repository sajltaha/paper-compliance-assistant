# Relevance evidence protocol

Task: PCA-22. This protocol was prepared before the discussions. Three dated participant records are now available; see INTERVIEW_FINDINGS.md and user_discussions_log.csv for findings and consent.

Accepted routes are 3–5 actual discussions with intended users, a substantive agreement with an actual client, or explicit permission from the receiving authority to substitute data analysis. A general positive supervisor response does not automatically establish client agreement or substitution permission.

Invite students or early-career researchers with manuscript-preparation experience. Obtain consent to anonymized notes first. Avoid requesting private manuscripts, contacts or unnecessary information. Use U01–U05 codes and record actual dates and roles.

1. When and for which submission stage did you last check requirements? Which sources did you use?
2. Show or describe the checking sequence without sharing private text. Where do difficulties arise?
3. Which constraints most often need rechecking: pages, sections, anonymity, template or semantic criteria?
4. What would make an automated result verifiable and useful? What should accompany a recommendation?
5. What restrictions apply to external AI transmission, history retention and deletion?
6. On a synthetic example, ask the participant to find the rule, evidence and next action. Record observed difficulties and explain the demonstration's limits.

After each discussion, separate observation from interpretation. Map each significant finding to FR/NFR and a decision: change, retain with a reason, or investigate later. The log records discussion ID, actual date, anonymized role, consent, source/private reference, observation, requirement mapping, decision and revision. Describe the limits of the small sample.

If substitution is chosen, retain the exact permission, actual date, authority and scope. Update the report after receiving the decision; until then that condition remains open.
